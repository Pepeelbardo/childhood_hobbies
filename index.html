Hobbies

Videogames

I remember spending entire Saturday afternoons playing with my sister.

My favorite memory was getting my first console as a birthday gift.

Hiking

My dad used to take me hiking every summer, and I remember I fell in creek once.

I remember we took my nanny once, and she passed in the middle of the hike.

Toys

Skateboard

I learned to skateboard at the local skate park, and I remember falling constantly.

My friends and I would spend hours there after school, racing down the ramps.

Roller skate

I got my roller skates for Christmas and immediately tried them out in the living room.

I remember skating around the block with my sister every afternoon that summer.
